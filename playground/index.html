    How it works

    Both texts are normalized (case, punctuation, contractions, fillers, number words), aligned once with a longest-common-subsequence pass, and judged by three independent checks. The thresholds come from a held-out benchmark, not intuition.

    Coverage

    How much of the source survives, and in what order: lexical coverage, order retention, and the longest dropped or moved block of content words.

    Novelty

    Every content word in the rewrite must come from the source or from evidence you supply. A new name, place or number fails on its own.

    Protected content

    Negations and numbers are claims. Dropping "not" or changing 40 to 45 fails no matter how high the coverage is.
